## Key handling rules

- Every key pair is generated by the CLI with `autogenerate-new-keypair save-to-legacy-keychain`, which writes it into the local credentials store (`~/.near-credentials/testnet/`). Nothing is typed, pasted, echoed, logged or committed.
- Every transaction is signed with `sign-with-legacy-keychain`. Never use `sign-with-plaintext-private-key`, `sign-with-seed-phrase`, `print-to-terminal`, `export-account` or `--teach-me` (it prints full RPC bodies).
- Never `cat`, `ls -la`, copy or upload files in the credentials store. Only public keys (`ed25519:...`) may appear in output, notes or `deployments/testnet.json`.
- If any CLI version prints a seed phrase or private key for a command below, stop, do not copy the output anywhere, and report it (UNVERIFIED: the docs show only "The data for the access key is saved in the keychain" for these commands).

### Registrar procedure for `resolve_written_off` and `resolve_stale_payout`

After the key is deleted the registrar is the only way to settle a quarantined forward or a stale payout lock that no real callback settled (THREAT_MODEL T12, T19, T19b). A decision moves money, so it follows these rules and no others.

1. Evidence is the final outcome of the original receipt on chain, found with the transaction hash of the payment (forward) or of the claim (payout): the receipt of the token `ft_transfer` the registry or the vault sent. Only a final outcome counts: success (the transfer happened) or failure (it did not). A receipt that is missing, still pending, or that you cannot find is NOT evidence. Wait, or leave it stuck. Never decide from a balance, from who owns the share, or from elapsed time.
2. The `doc_hash` is the sha256 (64 lowercase hex characters) of the receipt id text (base58 as the explorer shows it, no spaces or newline) that the decision cites. Keep a note with the transaction hash, the receipt id, the outcome and the block next to it. One call, one receipt id (the contract refuses a hash it has seen for the share; it cannot know that two hashes name the same receipt, so never cite one receipt twice).
3. Read the state at ONE block and keep it: `get_forward_watch(token_id)` (note `written_off` and `settle_seq`), `get_used_evidence(token_id)` and the explorer page of the original receipt. Before sending, confirm in the explorer that the callback receipt of that forward (the `on_forward` receipt on the registry) has NOT executed: if it has, the amount is already settled and your decision is not needed. Then call `resolve_written_off(token_id, delivered_amount, failed_amount, expected_seq, doc_hash)` with `expected_seq` = the `settle_seq` you read (RF-H1). If the call fails with "the quarantine changed since it was reviewed", a callback or another settlement landed after your review: read again from the start, do not retry the same numbers. A `doc_hash` already used for the share is refused, so a receipt cannot be settled twice. At most 64 hashes can be used per share. Set `delivered_amount` to what that receipt delivered and `failed_amount` to what it failed to deliver (u128 strings). Their sum must be at most `written_off` (`get_forward_watch`); what you do not name stays quarantined, and you settle it with a later call that cites its own receipt. After any call the callbacks of that quarantine are ignored (event `late_forward_callback_fenced`), so the remainder is settled only by the registrar.
4. A delivered amount for a share that is disputed or waiting for approval is refused. Settle the dispute or the approval first, or settle the failed part alone.
5. `resolve_stale_payout(token_id, paid, expected_since_block, doc_hash)` needs a lock older than 1200 blocks; `expected_since_block` is the `since_block` of `payout_in_flight` in `get_ledger`, read with your review (a different lock is refused), and confirm in the explorer that no `on_claim` callback receipt executed for it. `paid` is true only if the withdraw receipt succeeded. The balance shown in `payout_uncertain` is a hint, never the evidence.
6. Each call needs 1 yoctoNEAR (a full-access key of the registrar) and is public with its `doc_hash`. A wrong decision can pay a share twice or credit it more than its vault holds, up to the quarantined or locked amount (T12).
